LO 1
ON EXAM — 6 TIERS: Chromosomal → Gonadal → Hormonal → Morphological → Secondary sex → Brain/behavior. DEFAULT = FEMALE. SRY (not Y itself) determines sex. Sertoli FIRST (AMH), Leydig SECOND (T). Estrogen closes epiphyses in BOTH sexes. Aromatase paradox — male brain masculinized by E2; α-fetoprotein protects female fetus

Chromosomal level & SRY (LO 1)
Sex-determining Region of the Y chromosome. Encoded on the SHORT ARM (Yp). Encodes testis-determining factor (TDF), a transcription factor
SOX9 — pushes the bipotential germinal ridge toward testis fate (Sertoli-cell commitment)
WNT4, RSPO1, FOXL2 — default ovarian pathway. Why "default is female" at every downstream tier
TURNER syndrome. Streak gonads (accelerated follicular atresia) → no hormones. Female phenotype with short stature, webbed neck, bicuspid AV, horseshoe kidney, lymphedema at birth
KLINEFELTER syndrome. SRY present → male phenotype. Extra X impairs seminiferous tubules → infertility, small firm testes, tall stature, gynecomastia, learning difficulties
DE LA CHAPELLE syndrome (46,XX male). Y→X SRY translocation during paternal meiosis → phenotypic male with testes. Typically infertile
SWYER syndrome. Loss-of-function SRY mutation → streak gonads (no testis). Female phenotype. Y-bearing gonads carry GONADOBLASTOMA risk → must be surgically removed
Gonadal differentiation (LO 1)
Mesodermal tissue on the MEDIAL side of the mesonephros (proto-kidney). Forms around week 5. Truly bipotential
Sertoli FIRST (secretes AMH; Müllerian regression begins early). Leydig SECOND (secretes T; stabilizes Wolffian). Order matters — a Sertoli-only defect can leave 46,XY with a persistent uterus (PMDS)
Granulosa + theca cells form around primordial germ cells. Fetal ovary is ENDOCRINOLOGICALLY QUIET — no AMH, no T
Hormonal tier — T vs AMH vs DHT (LO 1)
(1) Stabilizes Wolffian → SEED (epididymis, vas, seminal vesicles, ejaculatory duct). (2) Paracrine — each testis masculinizes IPSILATERAL duct. (3) Drives male brain patterning AFTER local aromatization to E2
Regresses Müllerian ducts → no uterus, tubes, or upper 1/3 vagina in males. Loss → PMDS (46,XY with uterus at hernia repair)
Virilizes EXTERNAL genitalia (penis, scrotum, prostate) + pattern hair. Made from T in genital skin, prostate, hair follicles
Secondary sex + brain/behavior tiers (LO 1)
Drives epiphyseal CLOSURE in BOTH sexes. Without estrogen (aromatase deficiency XX or XY; ER mutations) → long bones keep growing → tall stature + osteoporosis
T masculinizes the male brain only AFTER local conversion to ESTRADIOL in hypothalamus. Estrogen is the actual masculinizing signal — counterintuitive but consistent
α-fetoprotein binds maternal and fetal estrogens → keeps them out of the female fetal brain. Prevents inadvertent masculinization
Perinatal androgen (aromatized locally to E2) triggers APOPTOSIS of AVPV kisspeptin neurons in males. AVPV mediates positive feedback → males cannot mount an LH surge. Arcuate (negative feedback) preserved
ORGANIZATIONAL = perinatal, PERMANENT (brain structure, AVPV apoptosis). ACTIVATIONAL = adult, REVERSIBLE (libido tracks current T/E2; mood tracks menstrual E2; postpartum mood disorders from steroid withdrawal)

Müllerian vs Wolffian derivatives (LO 2)

T vs DHT territories (LO 2)

LO 3
ON EXAM — Pulsatile GnRH only. Two-cell model: LH→THECA→androgens; FSH→GRANULOSA→aromatase→E2. Granulosa LACKS 17α-hydroxylase. Moderate E2 = negative; sustained E2 >200 pg/mL × 48 h = positive (AVPV kisspeptin). P BLOCKS positive. α-subunit shared LH/FSH/TSH/hCG
HPG pipeline (LO 3)
GnRH (decapeptide) released in PULSES every 60-90 min. Cell bodies in MEDIAL PREOPTIC AREA + arcuate nucleus
LH + FSH (glycoprotein heterodimers). α-subunit SHARED with TSH and hCG
Theca + granulosa cells (two-cell unit) → E2, progesterone, inhibin A (luteal) or inhibin B (follicular)
GnRH details (LO 3)
10 amino acids (decapeptide). Released from median eminence axon terminals into hypophyseal portal system
Every 60-90 min in females. ARCUATE kisspeptin drives tonic pulses; AVPV drives pre-ovulatory surge
SLOW pulses → FSH-dominant. FAST pulses → LH-dominant. Part of why PCOS has LH > FSH
Gonadotropes DOWNREGULATE GnRH receptors and desensitize → axis shutdown after initial "flare". LEUPROLIDE depot for prostate CA, endometriosis, precocious puberty, IVF. Flare covered with anti-androgen in prostate CA
LH/FSH/TSH/hCG all share α-subunit. hCG cross-reacts at LH-R (IVF trigger + CL rescue) AND TSH-R (gestational thyrotoxicosis)

Kisspeptin (LO 3)
Anteroventral periventricular nucleus (within medial POA). STIMULATED by sustained high E2 → positive feedback → LH surge. Apoptosed perinatally in males from local T→E2 conversion → males can't mount surge
Arcuate nucleus. INHIBITED by E2 → mediates negative feedback + tonic GnRH pulse generation. PRESERVED in males
Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism (no puberty). Inability to activate GnRH neurons → low LH/FSH → low gonadal steroids

Meiotic arrest (LO 4)
Prophase I (DIPLOTENE stage). Begins in fetal life (~20 weeks gestation). Resolves only at LH surge → completes meiosis I → secondary oocyte + first polar body
Metaphase II. Arrests immediately after ovulation. Resolves at FERTILIZATION → completes meiosis II → second polar body + haploid egg
Longer meiosis I arrest (decades in older mothers) → cumulative damage → ↑ non-disjunction at meiotic resumption → aneuploidies (trisomy 21 most common, also 18, 13). Declining AMH reflects shrinking antral pool

Dominant follicle selection (LO 4)
Luteal-phase DROP in inhibin A (from involuted CL) removes FSH brake → FSH rises → recruits next cohort of antral follicles
Winner secretes rising E2 + inhibin B → these suppress FSH → weaker follicles (less FSH-sensitive) lose FSH → undergo ATRESIA
Clomiphene (SERM) blocks hypothalamic ER → removes negative feedback → FSH stays HIGH → multiple follicles escape atresia. Letrozole (AI) lowers E2 → same FSH rise. Explains ↑ DIZYGOTIC TWINS
Relatively LOW FSH + excess theca androgens → multiple follicles arrested at antral stage → NO dominant follicle → chronic anovulation → “string of pearls” (>12 small antral follicles on US)
AMH as ovarian reserve (LO 4)
SMALL ANTRAL granulosa cells (primary + secondary + early antral). Serum AMH correlates with antral pool size
(1) Diminished ovarian reserve / POI. (2) Menopause approaching. Also: chemo/radiation damage
PCOS — many arrested small antral follicles. AMH is high despite anovulation
Predicts ovarian RESPONSE to stimulation and doses gonadotropins. Also helps with fertility counseling for delayed childbearing
Corpus luteum (LO 4)
LH SURGE triggers it. Granulosa + theca cells HYPERTROPHY, acquire LIPID DROPLETS, become yellow. Blood vessels invade previously avascular follicle → high hormone output
P = Progesterone (DOMINANT). E = Estradiol. I = Inhibin A. Main 3 CL products
~12 days without hCG rescue. Then involutes → corpus albicans → P/E2 fall → endometrium sloughs → MENSES
Trophoblast-secreted β-hCG binds LH RECEPTOR on CL (shares α with LH + near-identical β). Keeps P output sustained. hCG detectable 8-10 days post-ovulation, doubles every 48 h
~8-10 weeks gestation. Placental syncytiotrophoblast makes enough P to maintain pregnancy. After this, OOPHORECTOMY tolerated without miscarriage. hCG peaks ~10 weeks then declines

Follicular phase details (LO 5)
Loss of inhibin A from involuted CL (luteal → early follicular) removes FSH brake → FSH rises → recruits new cohort
Dominant follicle secretes escalating E2 + inhibin B → suppress FSH (atresia of competitors). E2 crosses surge threshold (~200 pg/mL × 48 h) → imminent positive feedback
Straight tubular glands; lengthening spiral arteries; mitotic activity; stratum functionalis re-growth driven by estradiol
Surge lasts ~24-48 h. OVULATION occurs ~36 h after LH begins rising, ~10-12 h after LH PEAK
Granulosa (LH-R positive) make LOCAL PROGESTERONE → activates proteolytic enzymes (collagenase, plasminogen activators) → wall weakens. Thin-walled “stigma” bulges on surface → ruptures → ovum + cumulus released
Luteal phase details (LO 5)
Day 21-22 (mid-luteal). ~7 days after ovulation
Day-21 serum P >5-10 ng/mL = CONFIRMS an ovulatory cycle
Tortuous + sacculated GLANDS; glycogen-rich cells; SUBNUCLEAR VACUOLES appear days 16-18 (“early secretory”); spiral arteries coil; stroma DECIDUALIZES
PROGESTERONE BLOCKS positive feedback at AVPV hypothalamus + pituitary — even though E2 still high during luteal phase, P prevents a second surge. Ensures one-ovulation-per-cycle
No pregnancy → CL lacks sustained LH → involutes after ~12 days → P + E2 FALL → spiral arteries CONSTRICT → endometrial ischemia + cell death → sloughing + menses. Loss of inhibin A allows FSH to rise → next cycle

Pregnancy & luteoplacental shift (LO 5)
Detectable ~8-10 days post-ovulation. Doubles every 48 h in normal early pregnancy

PCOS deep dive (LO 6)
2 of 3: (1) Oligo/anovulation (oligomenorrhea or amenorrhea). (2) Clinical or biochemical HYPERANDROGENISM (hirsutism, acne, ↑ free T). (3) POLYCYSTIC OVARIES on US (≥12 small antral follicles, “string of pearls”)
Fast GnRH pulses favor LH > FSH. High LH stimulates thecal androgens; relatively low FSH means granulosa aromatase underactive → follicles arrest at antral stage → no dominant follicle
Peripheral ADIPOSE TISSUE aromatizes androstenedione → estrone. Chronic anovulation means NO CL = NO PROGESTERONE. Chronic low-level E without P → endometrial hyperplasia → cancer
Hyperinsulinemia stimulates theca androgen production AND lowers hepatic SHBG → more free T. Self-reinforcing loop. Metformin addresses IR
LETROZOLE (aromatase inhibitor) — now first-line. Blocks peripheral aromatization → lower E2 → more FSH. Clomiphene (SERM) is the traditional alternative
SPIRONOLACTONE — AR antagonist (off-target). TERATOGENIC to male fetus → use reliable contraception (combined OCP)
Menopause & HRT (LO 6)
Inhibin B falls first (declining follicle pool). Then FSH rises (loss of inhibin). Eventually ovary can't make E2 → full menopause
Loss of ovarian INHIBIN (which selectively suppresses FSH) adds to loss of E2 negative feedback. Both rise, but FSH rises MORE (lost both brakes)
Peripheral aromatization of adrenal ANDROSTENEDIONE → ESTRONE (E1) in adipose tissue + bone. Only source. Obesity → more aromatase → ↑ endometrial/breast cancer
H = Hot flashes / vasomotor. A = Atrophy of vagina. V = Vaginal dryness. O = Osteoporosis. C = Coronary artery disease risk. S = Sleep disturbance, mood, cognitive fuzziness
Uterus INTACT → estrogen + progestin (protects endometrium from unopposed-E hyperplasia/cancer). Post-HYSTERECTOMY → estrogen alone
HRT risk-benefit most favorable when STARTED <60 years OR within 10 years of menopause. Starting late (10+ years post) has increased CV + stroke risk

10 vignettes — answers pre-revealed.
Q1. 16-year-old phenotypically female with primary amenorrhea, normal breasts, sparse axillary/pubic hair, blind-ending vagina. 46,XY. Bilateral inguinal masses. ↑ T, ↑ LH, ↑ E2. No uterus. Diagnosis?
A. Swyer syndrome
B. 5α-RD deficiency
C. Complete AIS — X-linked AR loss-of-function
D. PMDS
E. 21-OH deficiency
C — Complete AIS.
46,XY with functional Sertoli makes AMH → Müllerian regression (no uterus, blind vagina). Leydig makes T but AR nonfunctional → Wolffian regresses, external defaults female, no pubic hair (AR-dependent). Peripheral AROMATASE converts T → E2 → ERα drives breast development. AR on X chromosome → X-linked recessive.
Q2. 3-day-old 46,XX infant with ambiguous genitalia, vomiting, hypotension, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia. Elevated 17-OH-progesterone. Diagnosis?
A. Turner
B. 21-OH deficiency CAH
C. 5α-RD
D. CAIS
E. Aromatase deficiency
B — 21-OH deficiency CAH.
Can't make cortisol or aldosterone → substrate shunts to androgens in 46,XX → in-utero virilization. Normal uterus + ovaries. Aldo deficiency → salt-wasting crisis. Elevated 17-OH-progesterone is diagnostic. Emergency: IV hydrocortisone + fluids + electrolytes + later fludrocortisone.
Q3. 46,XY infant born with female-appearing external. Normal testes, vasa deferentia, epididymides, NO uterus. At puberty: phallic growth, muscle, voice deepening. Normal T, LOW DHT, ↑ T:DHT ratio. Enzyme?
A. 17,20-lyase
B. Aromatase
C. 5α-reductase type 2
D. 21-OH
E. 17β-HSD3
C — 5α-RD2 deficiency.
5α-RD2 converts T → DHT in peripheral tissues (genital skin, prostate, hair follicles). No DHT → external masculinization fails in utero → female-appearing at birth. Wolffian (T-dependent, not DHT) preserved. Puberty: rising T partially virilizes externally → “guevedoces.” Labs: normal T, LOW DHT, ↑ T:DHT.
Q4. Two-cell / two-gonadotropin model — correct pairing?
A. Theca expresses aromatase
B. Granulosa expresses 17α-hydroxylase
C. LH → theca → androstenedione; FSH → granulosa → aromatize androstenedione → estrone → 17β-HSD1 → estradiol
D. FSH → theca; LH → granulosa
E. Both cells make E2 independently
C — LH/theca + FSH/granulosa.
Thecal cells have 17α-OH + 17,20-lyase → androstenedione under LH. LACK aromatase. Granulosa has FSH-driven aromatase but lacks 17α-OH. Androstenedione diffuses theca → granulosa → aromatized to estrone → 17β-HSD1 → estradiol. Mnemonic LAT/FAG.
Q5. Sustained elevation of E2 >200 pg/mL for ~48 h triggers LH surge. Which population mediates positive feedback, and why can't males mount a surge?
A. Arcuate kisspeptin; males have apoptosed arcuate
B. AVPV kisspeptin; in males, perinatal androgens aromatized to E2 trigger APOPTOSIS of AVPV neurons
C. PVN GnRH; males lack PVN
D. SON vasopressin; suppressed in males
E. No hypothalamic mediator
B — AVPV kisspeptin; apoptosed perinatally in males.
AVPV (in POA) mediates POSITIVE feedback; sustained high E2 activates it → GnRH burst → LH surge. Arcuate kisspeptin mediates NEGATIVE feedback + tonic pulses. In males, perinatal androgens aromatized to E2 in hypothalamus trigger apoptosis of AVPV. Arcuate preserved. Aromatase paradox — actual masculinizing signal is E2.
Q6. 29-year-old with oligomenorrhea, hirsutism, acne, BMI 34. LH:FSH 2.8, ↑ free T, mildly ↑ DHEA-S, “string of pearls” on US. Why is FREE T elevated despite normal total?
A. Anabolic steroids
B. Adrenal androgen tumor
C. Hyperinsulinemia + obesity suppress hepatic SHBG → raise free T
D. 21-OH deficiency
E. Pregnancy
C — Hyperinsulinemia suppresses SHBG.
PCOS hyperandrogenism has two drivers: (1) ovarian thecal overproduction (LH:FSH >2 + insulin co-stim of theca); (2) hyperinsulinemia + obesity SUPPRESS hepatic SHBG → higher free T fraction. Metformin and weight loss raise SHBG. Rotterdam criteria met.
Q7. 22-year-old distance runner, BMI 17, 10 months amenorrhea, stress fractures, low BMD. LH 1.2, FSH 2.8, E2 18 pg/mL. Normal PRL, TSH. Diagnosis + mechanism?
A. POI
B. Prolactinoma
C. FHA — low leptin → low kisspeptin → low GnRH
D. PCOS
E. Kallmann
C — FHA (female athlete triad).
Low energy availability (BMI 17 + training) → LOW LEPTIN → reduced kisspeptin drive → reduced GnRH → Lo-Lo pattern. Triad = low energy + menstrual dysfunction + low BMD. POI would show HIGH FSH/LH; prolactinoma ↑ PRL; PCOS has high LH + androgens; Kallmann has anosmia. Treat with behavioral intervention first.
Q8. 37-year-old woman, 9 months amenorrhea, hot flashes, vaginal dryness. FSH 68, LH 42, E2 12, normal TSH/PRL. 45,X/46,XX mosaic. Fragile X premutation positive. Diagnosis + mechanism?
A. Menopause (too young to matter)
B. POI — accelerated follicular atresia
C. Kallmann
D. Turner classic (45,X)
E. CAIS
B — POI.
POI = menopausal labs (FSH >40) + amenorrhea before age 40. Causes include fragile X premutation, Turner mosaic, autoimmune, chemo/radiation, galactosemia, idiopathic. Hi-Lo pattern: loss of ovarian inhibin + E2 → FSH/LH rise. TX: HRT until ~51 to protect bone + CV. Fertility counseling. Workup: karyotype, fragile X, autoimmune.
Q9. 29-year-old undergoes endometrial biopsy day 22 for infertility. Shows tortuous sacculated glands, glycogen-rich cells, subnuclear vacuoles, decidualized stroma. Dominant hormone?
A. Estradiol
B. FSH
C. Progesterone from the corpus luteum
D. Prolactin
E. hCG
C — Progesterone from the CL.
Day 22 = mid-luteal. SECRETORY phase endometrium (glandular, tortuous, glycogen-rich, decidualized) driven by P from CL. Subnuclear vacuoles days 16-18 (“early secretory”). This biopsy CONFIRMS ovulation (functioning CL). Endometrial biopsy is one way to confirm ovulation; serum day-21 P >5-10 ng/mL is the standard test.
Q10. 58-year-old postmenopausal woman, intact uterus, considering HRT for vasomotor symptoms. Why combined estrogen-progestin rather than estrogen alone?
A. Progestin amplifies vasomotor relief
B. Progestin prevents estrogen VTE
C. Progestin opposes estrogen's proliferative effect on ENDOMETRIUM — reduces hyperplasia + adenocarcinoma risk
D. Progestin required for bone
E. Estrogen alone causes hot flashes
C — Progestin protects endometrium.
Unopposed estrogen drives endometrial proliferation → hyperplasia → adenocarcinoma. Uterus-intact → HRT MUST include progestin. Post-hysterectomy can use estrogen alone. Timing hypothesis: HRT risk-benefit best when started <60 or within 10 years of menopause. VTE + breast CA risks intrinsic to estrogen. Same principle in PCOS (unopposed estrone from aromatase without P).
